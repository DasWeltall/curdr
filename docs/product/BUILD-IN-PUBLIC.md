# Seven days building curdr in public

The challenge: seven days, three short videos per day, one useful product improvement per day. Three videos are three angles on the same evidence, not three new features. Record one screen capture and reuse it; batch editing so content does not consume the build.

Primary product target: get five solo founders to connect their own Hermes and finish one real brain dump; see whether at least three return voluntarily the next day. These are targets, not existing users or guaranteed results. Followers and views are secondary. Model usage/hosting may cost money even though curdr's code is free.

## Daily rhythm

Morning: the problem and today's falsifiable goal. Afternoon: one actual implementation, trade-off or failure. Evening: a live test/result and one specific request for feedback. Keep clips around 15–30 seconds. No fake success, staged user testimonials, secrets or screenshots of private brain dumps. Ask testers to describe setup failures without sharing keys or personal data.

| Day | Problem / morning hook | Build / afternoon hook | Test / evening hook | Proof to capture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | “I have 20 ideas. The problem is choosing one.” | “My AI turned a brain dump into more work. I changed that.” | “Which of these would you actually do tomorrow?” | Original dump → two actions + parked ideas |
| 2 | “I don't need an AI that loves every idea.” | “I gave it an idea I probably shouldn't build.” | “Did it challenge me, or just sound confident?” | Reality check, weak assumption, cheap test |
| 3 | “If I explain my startup again, this isn't helping.” | “Here's what my agent should remember. And what it shouldn't.” | “I changed the goal. Did the old advice disappear?” | First-run interview, edited fact, scoped recall |
| 4 | “Proactive AI gets annoying very quickly.” | “Nothing changed. So why did it message me?” | “This is the only interruption I'd have wanted today.” | Silent unchanged check vs a sandbox deadline change |
| 5 | “Thinking isn't a result.” | “Show me what the agent actually did.” | “It can draft this. Sending still needs my approval.” | Real tool trace, saved file, exact approval |
| 6 | “An Android APK doesn't help my iPhone friends.” | “One link. My Hermes installs its own interface.” | “Can someone else install this without me explaining it?” | HTTPS PWA on a tester phone, honest setup failures |
| 7 | “I built it. That doesn't mean it's useful.” | “Here's what testers actually got stuck on.” | “The source is public. What should earn a place next?” | Public repository, actual test results, remaining limits |

## Conversion that matches the stage

Send viewers to the product page, then its Copy installation message button. The message links to `install.md`; testers can paste it into their own Hermes. Use one clear CTA per video: try the install, test one behavior, or name one specific missing capability. Don't ask simultaneously for a follow, like, comment, share and signup.

Manually count: unique installation attempts reported, successful pairings, completed brain dumps, next-day returns and the most repeated blocker. Keep a private tester log with permission; never post raw personal content. The website currently has no analytics, waitlist or automatic DM workflow. Do not claim those funnels are tracking conversions.

Each night choose the next change from the strongest repeated failure. A dashboard that looks good on camera is less valuable than a setup fix that lets a real founder try the product. Three posts per day are an experiment, not a promise of distribution. If the format becomes filler, ship fewer stronger clips and keep testing the product.

## Day-one launch wording

I'm calling myself a content creator, but look at my follower count. I need to change something. So for the next seven days, I'm posting three videos a day while building an open-source AI co-founder for solo founders. Something I can brain dump into, that challenges weak ideas and actually helps with the work. I'm building it on Hermes. You can test it and help decide what belongs in it.

Read it at your natural pace and cut before recording if it exceeds 30 seconds. Show the product during the promise, not only a talking head. This is a draft; no posts have been published by this project setup.
