## Logo assets

Deliver a vector master plus monochrome light/dark versions. Export a 1024 × 1024 opaque PNG for `assets/icon.png`. Keep the symbol comfortably inside the canvas, without manually rounding the outside corners. Test against the actual light and dark app backgrounds.
