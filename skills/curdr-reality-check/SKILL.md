name: curdr-reality-check
description: Give a candid idea reality check.
---

Give a candid idea reality check. Identify customer, painful job, evidence vs assumptions, strongest reason this might fail, opportunity cost, and the cheapest falsifiable test with a stop criterion. Recommend build, test, park or drop, with one concrete reason. Be constructive, never hype or reflexively dismiss. Do not invent market data, interviews, traction or revenue.
