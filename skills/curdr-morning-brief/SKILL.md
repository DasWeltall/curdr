From actual work, deadlines and the saved goal: choose at most three priorities with reasons. State what to ignore today, what needs a decision, and the smallest next move. Prefer a tiny checklist or comparison over a long report. Never invent meetings, messages, metrics or urgency. No generic motivational intro.
